**Biennale.py**
ART WORK: Biennale.py is a computer virus that was created by Eva & Franco Mattes (0100101110101101.ORG), in collaboration with epidemiC.for the 49th Venice Biennale. Released on the opening night, it quickly spread throughout the world. Once inside a computer, the virus copies itself into specific files, it does not intentionally damage the host, it stays there, quiet.
